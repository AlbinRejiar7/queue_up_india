Overview

QueueUp India ("we", "our", "us") is a gaming matchmaking and chat app. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices. By using QueueUp India, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

  • Account data: phone number or Google sign-in details.
  • Profile data: username, avatar, and preferences.
  • Game preferences: game, rank, language, and availability status.
  • Chat data: direct and party messages you send or receive.
  • Device data: device identifiers, OS version, and app version.
  • Notification tokens: FCM tokens for push notifications.
  • Crash diagnostics: Crashlytics logs for stability and fixes.

How We Use Data

  • Provide matchmaking, parties, and chat features.
  • Show availability and player discovery.
  • Send notifications and updates.
  • Improve app performance and reliability.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, or harmful behavior.

How We Share Data

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with trusted service providers needed to operate the app, including Firebase (Google) for authentication, database, messaging, and crash reporting. We may also share data to comply with legal requirements.

Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account. Some records may be retained for legal, security, or operational reasons.

For detailed account deletion steps, see: Delete Account.

Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Your Rights
