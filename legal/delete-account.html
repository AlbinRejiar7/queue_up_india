Delete in the App

  1. Open QueueUp India.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap "Delete Account".
  4. Confirm the deletion.

Your account is deleted immediately after confirmation.

Delete by Email (if you cannot access the app)

Email sprt.queueupindia@gmail.com with the subject line Delete Account Request and include:

  • Your phone number (with country code) or Google email.
  • Your QueueUp India display name (if available).

We will process email requests within 7 days.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Account profile (name, avatar, preferences).
  • Availability status and match preferences.
  • Push notification tokens.
  • Login credentials linked to QueueUp India.

What Data May Be Retained

  • Messages you sent may remain visible to other users as part of their conversation history.
  • Reports you submitted or moderation logs may be retained for safety and legal purposes.
